# RVMKK attack artwork prompts

These animation atlases were created with the built-in image generation tool, using only each fighter's existing combat artwork as the identity and costume reference. The original images are retained. The runtime samples the atlas frames directly; no Python image editing was used.

## keen-kahn

Reference: `public/assets/rvmkk-rebuild/combat/keen-kahn.png`

Final atlas: `public/assets/rvmkk-rebuild/combat/attacks/keen-kahn.png`

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: a real fighting-game animation sprite atlas, on a genuinely transparent background.
Use the supplied existing full-body fighter as the ONLY identity and costume reference. Make a 2 by 2 grid of FOUR full-body animation frames of THIS SAME fighter, no other people. The four cells are exactly equal, with no visible grid, labels, borders, scenery, shadow floor, text or watermark. Square atlas, four square cells.
All four frames show the fighter facing RIGHT, in a consistent side-on three-quarter view so his/her face is still recognizable. Keep camera, scale, lighting and rendering style identical across cells. The planted foot is at 40% of cell width and 92% of cell height; the top of the head/hat is around 10% of cell height. Every hand, foot and weapon must remain fully inside its own cell, with transparent gutters. Natural limb proportions. Preserve the exact reference face, age, hair, skin, outfit, armor, colors, marks, emblems and identifying props. Slightly realistic caricature, matching the reference.
TOP LEFT: punch wind-up: elbow bent, striking fist chambered near chest, free hand guards.
TOP RIGHT: punch impact: clearly extend the striking upper arm and forearm HORIZONTALLY toward the RIGHT into a straight punch at chest height, fist fully extended. Show actual changed elbow and shoulder positions, NOT just a tilted torso.
BOTTOM LEFT: kick wind-up: lift the striking knee toward the chest, balancing on the other planted foot, hands guard.
BOTTOM RIGHT: kick impact: extend that leg HORIZONTALLY to the RIGHT into a strong waist-height side/front kick with the foot fully out, knee opened and support leg planted. Show actual changed hip and knee positions, NOT just a tilted torso.
The result will be UV-sampled as four separate frames, so no action crosses a cell edge and absolutely no background or framing elements. No dragons, added characters, alternate costumes, extra limbs, or redesigned faces.
FIGHTER: KEEN-KAHN. Preserve his exact broad face, cheek shape, short dark goatee, confident smirk and complexion. His ornate spiked GOLD CROWN stays on his head in every frame. Preserve ALL black-and-gold lion-head shoulder armor, R chest emblem, gold-edged hanging waist panel, tattooed upper arms, black/gold lion knee armor, boots and gold rings. Exactly two arms and two legs. The strike is a clenched gold-ringed fist; his costume is not to be simplified or redesigned.
```

## Keen-Kahn layout correction

The initial sheet placed the bottom-row crown too close to the upper-row boots. A final image edit re-spaced the same four poses into equal cells with clear transparent gutters, preserving the same face, crown, armor, emblems, pose order, and colors. No grid or labels were added. UV pivots and ground anchors are recorded in AttackAtlasMetadata.mjs.
